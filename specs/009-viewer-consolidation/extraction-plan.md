# Cubistry source extraction

The owner selected Cubistry's production renderer as the shared viewer foundation. Source baseline: Cubistry `efafcc1d97687eccea0cde87a230f447ce1b6c66`. The earlier pilot's application-adapter strategy is superseded. Preserve native geometry, materials, textures, lighting, shadows, camera, gestures, inertia and timing, and retain the original as an independent acceptance oracle. Gizmo and integrated play are additional universal capabilities; they are not established Cubistry production features.

The production renderer must use the extracted `PhysicalCubeScene` and rendering dependency closure, rather than a parallel scene implementation. ReScript Core owns piece identity, legality and moves; the public shared contract consumes canonical wire and appearance data and application scene configuration directly. Canonical piece and sticker IDs remain strings. Renderable frames do not imply that a Core piece without stickers does not exist. Presentation and sensor orientation remain separate from puzzle state. Native motion timing remains caller-controlled; app-owned deadlines remain app-owned. Preserve source notices and identify all source modifications.

Acceptance requires source-derived scene, projected-image, stickerless, artwork and layer tests; string-ID, zero-sticker and atomic cancellation tests; no production import of Cubistry state/move mechanics; and browser traces comparing native and extracted viewers at the same pose, fixture, DPR and input for standalone and clock multi-cube, followed by Calendar 7. Green CI is not an owner visual or interaction approval. Existing acceptance fixtures stay intact. Consumer migrations follow the owner's ordered gates after the extraction's live owner acceptance.
