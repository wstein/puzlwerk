## Selection and closure

Recommend adapting the scene and `beginTurn` pivot from `physical-scene.ts`, with the geometry subset of `presentation-preset.ts` and `two-radius-body.ts`. The actual unmodified scene also imports `body-artwork.ts`, `layer-widths.ts`, `projected-uv.ts`, and `cut-faces.ts`; they are recorded as conditional adaptation candidates, not a claim that all their features are necessary for a fixed scene. A smaller adapted slice must remove unused features and imports deliberately and be reviewed against this closure. `physical-scene.ts` imports `Construction` from `appearance.ts` as a type only; the appearance module itself has runtime dependencies and is reference only. `index.ts` is a reference for lifecycle and turn scheduling; `motion-timing.ts` is reference only; timing ownership follows the November 10 owner decision in the specification. Neither reference-only file is proposed for wholesale copying.

The unmodified selected closure is **not standalone**: `physical-scene.ts`, `body-artwork.ts`, `layer-widths.ts`, and `projected-uv.ts` import `@cubistry/cube-core` (runtime helpers `cross`, `dot` and `demand`, and the type-only `Vec3`); `appearance.ts` also imports core, assets and `picture-mode.ts`. The full `index.ts` imports core, mechanics, assets, appearance, picture modes, facelet images, studio, input, lighting and rotation modules, each with their own closure. These are boundary dependencies, not silently selected files. Future work must design explicit render input/types and replace or separately trace any retained helper; no app/core closure or assets are authorized here. `three` 0.187.0 and its RoundedBoxGeometry addon are external dependencies of the unmodified candidate; transitive installed/bundled artifact inventory remains open. The proposal does not assert an executable closure without those adaptations.

Exclude play gestures and turn mapping, camera/orbit controls, appearance UI, URL choices, lighting switches, application host, assets loader, solver and scanner. Projected artwork, cut surfaces and layer profiles exist in selected source and are not automatically part of the fixed-scene scope; retain only if later justified. The signed turn axis/angle and endpoints are rendering input; the application/core remains authoritative. Selection does not authorize an implementation, package or consumer migration.
